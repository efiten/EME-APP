# EME APP 1.3.0-test1

Test release of 1.3.0: measure your horizon with your phone and load it into the app, and a margin for the beam of your dish.

## New
- My horizon: "Measure horizon" opens horizon.dl6zg.de, the horizon survey of ON6ZG, on your phone. Walk around and aim at the top of the trees and houses, tap Download JSON there, and "Load horizon" turns the survey into obstacles in the app. Check them, mark thin trees as partial and save. Directions you did not measure stay open, and the message tells you which. In the app and in the web app. (#103)
- My horizon: enter the diameter of your dish and the band you use it on. Half the beamwidth is then added above your horizon, so the edge of the beam is clear too: 1.35° for a 6 m dish on 1296 MHz. It is used for "moon clear of your horizon" in the Moon tab and for "Moon at your QTH" in Live CQ, only when you have an own horizon. Leave the diameter empty for Yagis. In the app and in the web app. (#101)

## Under the hood
- The web app at eme.on8ar.eu installs a new release within about 5 minutes instead of 15. (#102, #104)

## Testing
- Install this APK over 1.2.0. It must install as an update; settings, your own horizon and the HB9Q login are kept.
- Settings > My horizon > Edit: tap "Measure horizon", measure a few directions on horizon.dl6zg.de, tap Download JSON, go back to the app and tap "Load horizon". Check that the obstacles and the chart match what you see outside.
- Enter your dish diameter and band, save, and check the window "Clear of your horizon" in the Moon tab.

This is a pre-release. The download link for users keeps pointing to the last real release.
