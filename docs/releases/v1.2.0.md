# EME APP 1.2.0

Your own horizon in the app, and the EME APP as a web app for iPhone and other browsers at https://eme.on8ar.eu.

## New
- My horizon: in the settings you can enter your own horizon, a minimum elevation and obstacles (houses, trees) from azimuth to azimuth, blocked or partial. A chart shows your horizon with today's moon track. (#96, #97)
- Moon tab: shows whether the moon is clear of your horizon, partly behind it or behind it, and a new line with the next window in which the moon is clear of your horizon. Live CQ colours "Moon at your QTH" by your own horizon. Without an own horizon nothing changes. (#99)
- Web app at https://eme.on8ar.eu, for iPhone users and anyone with a browser: Moon with the world map, Deg with graph and calendar, Live CQ, the N0UK chat, and the HB9Q chat with your own HB9Q login (stored only in your browser). It has the same texts in the same 13 languages and the same calculations as the app. Live CQ and the N0UK chat are fetched once for all web users together, at the same intervals as the app. (#57-#63, #65, #69, #71, #77, #84-#86, #89-#95)
- Settings: a link "Release notes" opens the notes of the installed version. (#56)

## Fixed
- A new install no longer uses ON5GS / JO21SC as your own station. Until you save your callsign and locator, Moon, Deg and Live CQ ask you to set them. (#55)
- Lists with the newest message on top (HB9Q chat, Chat CQ, Live CQ, N0UK) stay at the top when new messages arrive, so you see them without scrolling. (#76)
- Live CQ: "updated" shows the time of the last fetch that worked, not of a failed one. (#75)
- Small phones and large fonts: time, azimuth and event labels on the Moon tab, the Chat CQ table, the Live CQ spotter and DT, the Deg condition and the N0UK sub-tab names no longer get cut off or broken over two lines. (#72, #73, #74, #82, #83)

## Under the hood
- The calculations of the app and the web app are checked against the same reference values, and a new layout test checks every screen in all 13 languages. (#54, #70, #80, #88)

## Updating
- Installs over 1.1.0; settings and HB9Q login are kept.
- If you never saved your own callsign and locator, enter them in the settings: the app no longer fills in ON5GS / JO21SC.
