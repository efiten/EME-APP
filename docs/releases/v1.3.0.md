# EME APP 1.3.0

Your own obstacles (trees, houses) measured with your phone, a margin for the beam of your dish, and Live CQ without stations whose moon is down.

## New
- My obstacles: "Measure obstacles" opens horizon.dl6zg.de, the horizon survey of ON6ZG, on your phone. Walk around and aim at the top of the trees and houses, tap Download JSON there, and "Load obstacles" turns the survey into obstacles in the app. Check them, mark thin trees as partial and save. Directions you did not measure stay open, and the message tells you which. In the app and in the web app. (#103, #111)
- My obstacles: enter the diameter of your dish and the band you use it on. Half the beamwidth is then added above your obstacles, so the edge of the beam is clear too: 1.35° for a 6 m dish on 1296 MHz. It is used for "Moon clear of your obstacles" in the Moon tab and for "Moon at your QTH" in Live CQ, only when you have entered obstacles. Leave the diameter empty for Yagis. In the app and in the web app. (#101)
- Live CQ and the world map on the Moon tab no longer show stations where the moon is below the horizon now: they cannot be worked via EME. A station without a usable locator stays in the list. In the app and in the web app. (#112)

## Changed
- "My horizon" is now called "My obstacles", and the Moon tab says "Moon clear of your obstacles" and "Clear of your obstacles", in all 13 languages. (#116)

## Fixed
- Chat CQ: the Hz column only shows a number written as Hz (after the mode or CFOM, or before "Hz"). Before, a frequency without a dot or a clock time like "until 0930" could end up as Hz; now the column shows "?" instead. In the app and in the web app. (#113)

## Under the hood
- The app is now open source under the GPL-3.0 license, the README is up to date with screenshots, and the web app at eme.on8ar.eu installs a new release within about 5 minutes. (#102, #104, #108, #109, #114, #115)
