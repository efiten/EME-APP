# EME APP 1.5.0

When the moon is up at your station and at another station at the same time: the mutual moon window for the next 30 days.

## New
- Moon tab: the button "Mutual window" opens a list of the next 30 days. Enter the locator of the other station and you see each window when the moon is up at both stations at the same time: at yours clear of your obstacles (and the margin of your dish), at the other station above the horizon. Each line gives the day, the local start and end time, the duration, the highest elevation the moon reaches at both, and the degradation on the band of the Deg tab. A window that is already running shows its real start, and the last one its real end, also after the 30 days. After a change to or from summer time a line names its own time zone. The last 5 locators are one tap away. In the app and in the web app; in the web app the calculation runs in the background, so the page does not freeze. (#120)
