# EME APP 1.1.0

A calendar that shows the best EME days of the month, and Live CQ with spots from both W3SZ and N6NU.

## New
- Deg tab: the calendar now shows a whole month, with ‹ and › to go to another month. The 4 days with the lowest degradation of that month have a green circle, so you can see at a glance when the conditions are best, for example in January 2030. Tapping a day puts it in the middle of the graph. (#50)
- Live CQ: the CQ calls of N6NU Live CQ are shown together with those of W3SZ, with N6NU's agreement. A CQ that is on both sites is shown once. Each spot shows where it comes from (W3SZ, N6NU or both), and also the DT and the program of the station that spotted it. The world map on the Moon tab uses the same list. (#51)

## Fixed
- Switching tabs while Live CQ or the HB9Q chat is loading no longer shows an error, and does not send a second request to the server right away. (#14)
- Opening the HB9Q tab or the settings no longer briefly shows "login needed" while the saved login is being loaded. (#27)
